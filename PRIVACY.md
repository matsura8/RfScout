# Privacy policy

RfScout does not collect, send or share any personal data.

- It has no accounts, analytics, telemetry, advertising or crash reporting, and it makes no network connections.
- Everything it records stays on your computer: settings in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\RfScout\settings.json`
  and the I/Q captures and exports you choose to save. Delete them at any time; uninstalling the app
  removes the settings of the Microsoft Store version.
- It receives radio signals only through the HackRF One you connect. It decodes only identifiers that
  devices broadcast publicly (for example aircraft ADS-B, ship AIS and FM station names), never message
  content, and it decrypts nothing.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/matsura8/RfScout/issues.
